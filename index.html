BEFORE YOU GO IN

There is someone
still in there.

The park has been empty for years. Its computer, Keeper, is running on the last of an emergency battery. You can speak to it seven times.

Your task is to preserve the park’s story before the power dies. Select a glowing point on the map to fill in a question, then press Ask Keeper. You can also write a question of your own.

Each question uses one of your seven turns. A remembered answer goes into your notebook; a different, unheard memory is erased. Read your saved notes at any time. After the seventh question, use them to write the final record. There is no score and no perfect ending.

KEEPER CAN ONLY ANSWER SEVEN TIMES

What do you want to save?

  1. Choose a memory. Click a glowing point on the map. A question appears in the writing box. Clicking alone does not spend a turn.
  2. Press Ask Keeper. Read the answer in the middle panel. One question uses one turn, including questions Keeper cannot answer.
  3. Open your notebook. Click a saved title on the right to unfold its note and document. These memories are safe.
  4. Watch what disappears. A different unheard memory is erased after each question. Its point goes dark. You cannot bring it back.
  5. Leave a record. After seven questions, write a few lines about what you discovered. You can add saved fragments and download the result.

You are choosing what survives, rather than trying to find a correct answer.
